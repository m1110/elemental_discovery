How much to trust it

Each law was fitted on lighter nuclei and asked to predict heavier ones it had not seen, the way the elements were actually discovered.

Cross sections and their null searches are hand-compiled from the discovery papers. Predictions come from a transparent macroscopic–microscopic model and are no substitute for microscopic calculations. Generated by tools/build_ui.py in the elemental_discovery repository.
